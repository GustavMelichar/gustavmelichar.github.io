It was a great pleasure to take part in the meeting of the DFG network EmbodiNet in beautiful Marburg on November 5 and 6. Many thanks to Prof. Henrik Göhle for hosting us and for the warm welcome.

Embodiment research keeps gaining momentum, and our discussions reflected this. We worked on bodily discomfort and on the lived body in the age of digitalization, two topics that bring phenomenological analysis, psychopathology, and philosophy of mind into close contact with contemporary questions about technology and everyday life.

I am grateful for the stimulating exchange and look forward to the next steps in the network.
