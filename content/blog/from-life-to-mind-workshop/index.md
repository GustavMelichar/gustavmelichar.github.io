I am pleased to announce the international workshop **"From Life to Mind: The Return of Hylemorphism in Embodied Cognition"**, taking place on **September 2–3, 2026** at the **Heidelberger Akademie der Wissenschaften**.

The workshop asks: Can the ancient doctrine of form and matter illuminate the contemporary science of embodied, enactive minds? Bringing together philosophers working in ancient philosophy, metaphysics, and philosophy of mind, the workshop explores whether hylemorphism — the Aristotelian framework of form and matter — offers conceptual resources for understanding cognition, life, and mind in a non-reductive way.
